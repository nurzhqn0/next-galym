# Surface brief: landing and public site

Scope: the whole public site under app/[lang]/ (landing, news, applications, archives, login, account). Mode: Persuade.

Audience and action: students, mostly on phones, who must find an open application and apply. Proof available today: live listings, news and archive from the database. Results figures, About text and Kazakh/Russian copy are not supplied: drafts are marked, numbers stay blank.

Chosen direction: The Answer Sheet (Impeccable's pick, not the roll). Memorable moment: applying is filling a bubble.

Unresolved: About copy, Results figures, program type, photos, kk/ru translations (drafted for approval).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an exam answer sheet and you apply by filling in a bubble. Printing grammar (numbered rows, A-D bubbles, comb-cell fields, timing track, registration marks) structures every section. It refuses the hero-plus-three-cards program site.

OWN-WORLD: Gold stock #ffd576 like a carbon-copy sheet, printed in one crimson ink #a8042b, body text in crimson-black #2a0610. Filled bubbles are solid crimson. One drenched crimson band printed in reverse (gold on crimson) for login and footer. Components: boxed comb-cell fields, bubble radios and buttons, a black timing-mark column on the left edge, registration crosshairs in corners, rubber-stamp status marks. Type: condensed grotesk caps for printed labels, a plain grotesk for reading, tabular figures; every face covers Kazakh letters.

STORY: A student sees within seconds that this is a program that takes applications, scans the open ones as numbered questions, and fills the bubble to apply. Trust comes from news, archive and results as they are supplied. Next step: sign in, apply, track status on the account page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed gold sheet, timing track on the left edge, crosshairs in the corners. Header is the sheet's field band: logo 01 at left, the seven nav items as boxed fields. Below it a very large condensed-caps headline across the width, then the open applications as numbered answer rows, each with an unfilled bubble; the primary action is the first row's bubble and an Apply button at 44px minimum height beside the headline. Empty state is a sheet of empty bubbles with plain text.

FORM: The Answer Sheet, position 1 of 7 on my ordered list (the roll assigned 5, Launch Day; the user chose this card). seed:0d4f4aa3953051fe
